# Antiphony · 對仗

**A visual philosophy of paired voices held in perfect counterweight.**

Antiphony is built on the oldest discipline of balance: two lines that answer each other, element for element, so that each becomes legible only through its partner. Form is never single. Every column has a twin across an invisible axis; every mark has a counter-mark that is its inverse — open against filled, light against weight — and meaning lives in the tension between them. The composition must feel as though a master had tuned each pair for days, adjusting a single disc by a hair until the two voices rang true together — meticulously crafted, the product of deep expertise.

Space is architectural and ceremonial. Columns stand in measured pairs, read from right to left, separated by gutters calibrated so that pairs feel bound and neighbouring pairs feel distinct. Above them runs one hairline — a ceiling — and the columns quietly rise past it, a small, deliberate transgression that gives the whole plate its pulse. Vast margins of untouched paper hold everything in suspension; nothing crowds, nothing leans.

Colour is a strict economy. Warm paper, carbon ink, and a vermilion used only where a line comes to rest — the point of resolution in each pair. A faint ochre may sit once as a seal of completion. Open circles are drawn with the same hairline care as filled ones; emptiness is treated as a positive mark, not an absence. Every tone should appear calibrated across countless sittings, nothing approximate.

Typography whispers in the margins like a prosodist's annotation: thin monospaced numerals, minute labels set exactly on their baselines, one pale specimen glyph allowed to stand at monumental scale as a ghost behind the structure. Words never explain the pattern; they only name its parts.

Hierarchy emerges from rhythm. The eye first feels the alternation of filled and open, then the vermilion points of rest, then the ceiling line, and last the marginal notations. The finished plate should read like a page from a treatise on equilibrium — proof that the invisible music of balanced language can be charted, mark by mark, through master-level execution and painstaking attention.
